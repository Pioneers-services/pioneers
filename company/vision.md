# Vision
Status: Agreed direction

Pioneers is Shaheen's business-services company, founded in 2019 and being rebuilt around industry-specific SaaS. Deliver good quality at accessible prices, with commercial customers first, not government procurement.

Expansion ambition: MENA → Africa → USA → global. This is strategic direction, not a committed launch calendar. Infrastructure investment grows with validated demand. A future Silicon Valley presence is an aspiration, not an approved expense.

## Brand architecture
- Pioneers: commercial company and trust/accountability owner.
- Carminta: proprietary AI engineer/orchestrator and envisioned character behind Pioneers products; not the ERP product.
- Connect X1 — by Carminta: first SaaS product, informed by the interior design, fit-out, and custom manufacturing MVP.
- Carminta X1: Connect's implementation and workflow assistant.

Products will be reached through pioneers.services; exact paths/subdomains are undecided. Carminta domains are optional and not a current priority. Do not claim a world-first, trademark clearance, or a proprietary trained foundation model without evidence.

# Memory and learning
Status: Agreed boundaries; detailed retention policy open

Business truth lives in authoritative database records. Retrieve current permitted data; never treat an old conversational memory as today's price or balance.

Separate product knowledge, company-confirmed preferences, conversation history and execution evidence. Memory entries carry provenance, scope, confidence/status, timestamps and retention class. Authorized administrators can inspect and correct important preferences. Personal/sensitive data must have appropriate access and deletion procedures.

Remember a verified outcome, not merely an attempted action. Learning produces hypotheses and proposed improvements; it cannot silently change business rules, permissions or financial records. Persistent workflow rules require approval and structured configuration.

Carminta releases reviewed/versioned playbooks, tools, templates and evaluations to daughters. Company memories are not automatically shared across customers or used for model training. Product improvement feedback must be authorized and minimized. Initial learning means knowledge/configuration improvement, not foundation-model retraining.

The public Carminta character has no direct customer-data access. Protect retrieval and tool use from instructions embedded in documents or external content.

# Connect X1 — by Carminta
Status: Agreed product direction; delivery scope pending approval

## Purpose
An accessible industry-specific SaaS that connects sales, pricing, design, production, delivery, and business oversight. Initial domain: interior design, fit-out, and custom furniture/manufacturing businesses similar to AKA.

## Rebuild principle
Use the existing AKA HTML and business experience as workflow/layout references, not architecture to copy. Preserve useful behavior through specifications and tests. Do not copy production data into this repository.

## Differentiators
- Industry-aware estimating and connected project workflows.
- Scanning and Action Center as core functionality.
- Carminta X1 helps configure company information, branches, roles, price lists, and approved workflow templates.
- Detect → explain → assign → propose → approve → execute → verify → remember → learn → propose improvements.

## Success
A company can be configured without a code fork, complete a real job reliably, and see evidence behind numbers and warnings. A second company can onboard without source edits and cannot access the first company's data.

## Claims to avoid
No claim of complete statutory accounting, country compliance, autonomous software development, scalability limits, or production readiness before corresponding implementation and verification.
